A NOTE FROM SALENE · YOU MAY TAKE A MAP, OR IGNORE IT COMPLETELY

Welcome to my world.

I made this place because I believe curiosity deserves somewhere to play. I like strange questions, small acts of care, and beautiful ordinary things. I am trying to live softly and bravely: to love people deeply, trust what I care about, do things scared, and let life change me.

This is my world made from those attempts. There is no right route through it. Follow what catches your attention. Take what you need. Leave with a new question.
