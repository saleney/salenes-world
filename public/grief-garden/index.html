About this garden

Richard loved juicing, farmers markets, oud, rose oil, his friends, and finding intimate underground music gatherings—whether in someone’s living room, on Third Street, at a warehouse, or in Chinatown in Oakland. He was a darling of a man, filled with boundless generosity, and made the best damn juice.

These are his recipes, handwritten by his dad. And this was the last song he played for us the last time we saw him.
