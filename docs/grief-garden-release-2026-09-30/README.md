# The Grief Garden — September 30, 2026 release

A garden drawn in code, guided by Salene’s Variant recordings. Thin curved stems carry fuller ingredient drawings. Plants sway individually and grow from their bases. Click an ingredient to plant randomly, or drag it into the garden; click its fruit/vegetable tip to harvest it into Your Harvest.

The campfire and basket are SVG companions. Campfire toggles the preserved Spotify song panel. About opens a soft panel with Salene’s exact Richard text. Morning, Afternoon 1 and Afternoon 2 select the original recipe ingredients; the original handwritten recipe photo and readable transcripts remain.

The background uses faint landscape layers, gradients, grain and warm firelight. Reduced-motion preferences disable animation. Plants and harvest are saved in the browser under a new living-garden key; the previous garden’s saved data is not overwritten. Starter plants refill when the page is reopened.
